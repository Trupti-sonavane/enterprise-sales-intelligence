## Advanced DAX Measures

### 1. YTD Sales
Calculates cumulative sales from the beginning of the year up to the selected date.

```dax
YTD Sales =
TOTALYTD(
    [Total Sales],
    'Date'[Date]
)
```

### 2. Previous Year Sales
Calculates sales for the corresponding period in the previous year.

```dax
Previous Year Sales =
CALCULATE(
    [Total Sales],
    SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR('Date'[Date])
)
```

### 3. YoY Growth %
Calculates the percentage increase or decrease in sales compared to the previous year.

```dax
YoY Growth % =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Sales] - [Previous Year Sales],
    [Previous Year Sales],
    0
)
```

### 4. Rolling 3-Month Average Sales
Calculates the average monthly sales over a rolling three-month period.

```dax
Rolling 3-Month Average Sales =
DIVIDE(
    CALCULATE(
        [Total Sales],
        DATESINPERIOD(
            'Date'[Date],
            MAX('Date'[Date]),
            -3,
            MONTH
        )
    ),
    3
)
```

### 5. Profit Margin %
Calculates profit as a percentage of total sales.

```dax
Profit Margin % =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Profit],
    [Total Sales],
    0
)
```

## Note
These measures are created for time-based sales analysis, year-over-year comparison, rolling trend analysis, and profitability measurement. Further validation is required to confirm calculation accuracy.
